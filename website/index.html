Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Technical details on 60 Hz hit-testing, WinRT SMTC media integration, dock geometry, and offline privacy.

How does EkPill let me click through the transparent window area at 60 Hz? +

EkPill creates a frameless always-on-top Qt6 QQuickView and runs a 16 ms (60 Hz) Win32 cursor hit-test loop. When your cursor is outside the live QML capsule bounding box, EkPill applies WS_EX_TRANSPARENT | WS_EX_LAYERED via SetWindowLongW, allowing clicks and scrolls to pass straight through to underlying desktop apps with zero latency.

What happens when I dock EkPill to the Right Screen Edge instead of the Top Edge? +

Switching the Screen Edge setting to Right smoothly relocates the capsule to the right monitor edge (58×178 px collapsed) with top and bottom concave fillets. Every glyph—including the dot-matrix clock, day badge, battery icon, and Wi-Fi indicator—stays completely upright with zero sideways text rotation.

How do the Now Playing media controls work? +

EkPill queries Windows Runtime GlobalSystemMediaTransportControlsSessionManager on a background thread to read track title, artist, album art thumbnail streams, and playback timeline from Spotify, Chrome, Apple Music, or VLC. You get full transport control (Play, Pause, Next, Previous) and interactive seek bar right in the capsule.

How does the two-step Safe Power Menu prevent accidental clicks? +

The Power Menu requires a two-step confirmation bar before executing actions like Shut Down or Restart. Clicking an action exposes the confirmation toggle with clear visual feedback, preventing accidental session termination or sleep.

Why is the EkPillSetup.exe installer 180 MB? +

EkPillSetup.exe (188,489,585 bytes) is a 100% self-contained Inno Setup + PyInstaller distribution. It bundles the Python 3.13 runtime, Qt6 / PySide6 QML libraries, Direct3D/OpenGL shaders, and WinRT bridges so media control, files hub, app launcher, and QML animations work out of the box on any clean Windows 10 or 11 PC.

Does EkPill make any network connections or collect telemetry? +

No. EkPill operates 100% offline. There are zero analytics SDKs, zero telemetry pings, and zero cloud accounts. All preferences (theme, screen edge, auto-collapse delay, start-with-Windows) are stored locally in ~/.ekpill_config.json.
