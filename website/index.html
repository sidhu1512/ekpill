Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about EkPill's 60 Hz click-through windowing, audio bridges, screen recording, and privacy.

How does EkPill let me click through the transparent window area at 60 Hz? +

EkPill creates a frameless always-on-top Qt6 QQuickView and runs a 16 ms (60 Hz) Win32 cursor hit-test loop. When your cursor is outside the live QML capsule's animated bounding box, EkPill applies WS_EX_TRANSPARENT | WS_EX_LAYERED via SetWindowLongW, allowing clicks and scrolls to pass straight through to underlying desktop apps with zero latency.

What happens when I dock EkPill to the Right Screen Edge instead of the Top Edge? +

Switching the Screen Edge setting to Right smoothly relocates the capsule to the right monitor edge (58×178 px collapsed) with top and bottom concave fillets. Every glyph—including the dot-matrix clock, day badge, battery icon, and Wi-Fi indicator—stays completely upright with zero sideways text rotation.

How do the Now Playing media controls and Per-App Audio Mixer work? +

EkPill queries Windows Runtime GlobalSystemMediaTransportControlsSessionManager on a background thread to read track title, artist, album art thumbnail streams, and playback timeline from Spotify, Chrome, Apple Music, or VLC. For audio routing, it binds directly to WASAPI IAudioEndpointVolume and IAudioSessionManager2 via pycaw so you can adjust or mute individual application audio streams inside the capsule.

How does the built-in 30/60 FPS Screen Recorder avoid corrupted files? +

EkPill captures the desktop via FFmpeg (ddagrab hardware duplication with automatic fallback to gdigrab + libx264 -preset ultrafast) into a crash-safe Matroska (.mkv) container while recording. When you stop the recording, it automatically remuxes the segments into a web-ready .mp4 (-movflags +faststart).

Why is the EkPillSetup.exe installer 180 MB? +

EkPillSetup.exe (188,489,585 bytes) is a 100% self-contained Inno Setup + PyInstaller distribution. It bundles the Python 3.13 runtime, Qt6 / PySide6 QML libraries, Direct3D/OpenGL shaders, WinRT & WASAPI bridges, and a dedicated ffmpeg.exe binary so screen recording, media control, and QML animations work out of the box on any clean Windows 10 or 11 PC.

Does EkPill make any network connections or collect telemetry? +

No. EkPill operates 100% offline. There are zero analytics SDKs, zero telemetry pings, and zero cloud accounts. All preferences (theme, screen edge, auto-collapse delay, start-with-Windows) are stored locally in ~/.ekpill_config.json.
